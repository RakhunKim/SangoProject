## Excluded items

1. SMOKE/ (approximately 2.5GB)
   - Full output of the 250-step preliminary smoke tests
   - As stated in the manuscript: "Smoke-test outputs are stored separately
     and are not included in the experimental analysis."
   - These are preliminary validation artifacts unrelated to the reported
     experimental results.

2. 03_runs/{LoRA,DoRA,OFT}/seed{42,43,44}/trainer_checkpoints/ (approximately 1.56GB total)
   - Rolling resumable checkpoints saved every 250 steps during training
     (including optimizer/scheduler/RNG state, used to resume interrupted runs)
   - All nine runs completed successfully (COMPLETED status), and these
     intermediate checkpoints are not used to reproduce the final results.
     The artifacts required for reproduction — best_adapter/, final_adapter/,
     and history_adapters/ — are all included in this repository.

3. data_build/after_laser.jsonl (approximately 476MB)
   - An intermediate snapshot from the data-cleaning pipeline, taken
     immediately after LASER-based filtering
   - Superseded by the final clean pool (item 4, also excluded) and
     reproducible from the original NLLB data using audit_dedup_and_laser.py.

4. data_build/final_clean_pool.jsonl (approximately 921MB)
   - The fully cleaned pool of parallel sentence pairs (n = 3,013,344)
   - Original source: allenai/nllb (eng_Latn-sag_Latn), cited in the
     manuscript with the exact revision used.
   - Excluded to reduce repository size, as it is a large derived
     intermediate file from a publicly available dataset.
